Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Mongoose Ventures Pty Ltd, trading as Commerce Cortex (“we”, “us” or “our”), respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website (commercecortex.net) and our apps, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. Information we collect

Contact and sign-up forms

When you submit a form on our website, we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number and message. Form submissions are stored using Google Forms (part of Google Workspace), so they are held on Google’s servers.

Website analytics

We use web analytics to understand how visitors use our website and to improve it:

  • Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, how you reached our site, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), and your device, browser and operating system.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics collects aggregated visit statistics. It does not use cookies.

We use this information in aggregate and do not use it to identify you.

Spam and security protection

Our forms are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a submission comes from a person rather than an automated program. To do this, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address and browser characteristics.

Our apps

When a merchant installs one of our apps, we collect only the data needed to operate it, which may include:

  • store and product catalogue data, and store content such as pages, used to provide app features (for example, chatbot answers or bundle suggestions);
  • configuration and settings data needed for core app functionality;
  • usage data, such as app activity and general usage statistics.

Our apps are designed to work with public store content. We do not collect store customers’ personal information unless it is required for app operation or support.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • respond to your enquiries and sign-up requests, and contact you about our products and services you have asked about;
  • provide, maintain and support our apps;
  • understand how our website is used and improve it;
  • protect our website and forms against spam and abuse;
  • meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We will only send you marketing emails if you have asked to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our website and services:

  • Google LLC: stores form submissions (Google Forms) and provides website analytics (Google Analytics). See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosts our website and provides spam protection (Turnstile) and web analytics.
  • Ecommerce platform providers (such as Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce and Shopify): to integrate and operate our apps.
  • AI model providers (such as OpenAI and Amazon Web Services): to generate responses in our AI apps.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

4. Overseas storage

Our service providers may store and process information outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries where they operate data centres. We use reputable providers with appropriate security and privacy safeguards.

5. Cookies and your choices

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser. You can:

  • block or delete cookies in your browser settings;
  • install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Blocking cookies will not stop our website or forms from working.

6. Data retention

We keep form submissions for as long as we need them to respond to you and to manage our relationship with you, and then delete them. Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings of our analytics providers. App data is kept while the app is installed and is deleted or anonymised when no longer needed, unless we must keep it to meet legal requirements.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure, including using encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limiting access to those who need it.

8. Access, correction and complaints

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing us (see below). Merchants remain responsible for their own customers’ data under their store’s privacy policy.

If you have a privacy concern or complaint, please contact us first and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page, with its effective date at the top.
